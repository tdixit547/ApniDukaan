📦 About the Project

ApniDukaan is a full-stack e-commerce web application built as a college project. It demonstrates the implementation of a modern online shopping platform with features like user authentication, product browsing, shopping cart, order management, and reviews.

The platform allows users to browse products across multiple categories, add items to cart, place orders with different payment methods, and leave reviews for purchased products.

❓ How to Use

  • 🔐
    Create an Account Click "Login" in the navbar, then select "Register Instead" to create a new account
  • 🛍️
    Browse Products Use category filters or search bar to find products. Click on any product for details.
  • 🛒
    Add to Cart Click "Add to Cart" on any available product. View your cart by clicking the cart icon.
  • 💳
    Checkout Open your cart, click "Proceed to Checkout", enter shipping details, and place your order.
  • 📦
    Track Orders Go to "Orders" page to view order status and history.
  • ⭐
    Leave Reviews Click on any product to open details, scroll down to write your review.
